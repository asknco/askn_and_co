FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who needs a transfer pricing report in India?

Any taxpayer that enters into international transactions with associated enterprises (and certain specified domestic transactions above the prescribed limit) must maintain transfer pricing documentation and file an accountant's report in Form 3CEB.

When is Form 15CB required?

Form 15CB is a Chartered Accountant's certificate on the taxability of a payment to a non-resident and the correct rate of TDS. It is required for many foreign remittances above the prescribed limits, along with Form 15CA.

We are setting up a GCC in Hyderabad. Can you help?

Yes. We advise on the entity structure, the transfer pricing model for the centre, registrations and ongoing tax, GST and company law compliance.

How can I avoid double taxation on foreign income?

India's tax treaties and domestic law provide relief for tax paid abroad. We review the applicable treaty and claim foreign tax credit in your return with the required Form 67.
