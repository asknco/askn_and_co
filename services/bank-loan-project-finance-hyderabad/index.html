FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What documents do banks need for a business loan?

Usually two to three years of audited financial statements and income tax returns, GST returns, bank statements, KYC of the business and promoters, projections and CMA data, and documents of the property offered as security. MSMEs should also have Udyam registration.

What is CMA data for a bank loan?

CMA (Credit Monitoring Arrangement) data is the standard format banks use to assess loans. It presents past and projected operating statements and balance sheets, fund flow and key ratios such as the current ratio and debt service coverage ratio.

Can an MSME get a business loan without collateral?

Yes. Eligible micro and small enterprises can obtain collateral-free loans covered by a guarantee under the CGTMSE scheme, up to the scheme limit, subject to the bank's appraisal.

How long does a bank take to sanction a business loan?

It depends on the lender and the size of the loan. A complete, well-prepared proposal with CMA data and documents ready is the single biggest factor in reducing the time to sanction.

Do you guarantee that the loan will be approved?

No. The lending decision rests with the bank or NBFC. We prepare a complete and accurate proposal, present it professionally and resolve the lender's queries, which improves the chances and the speed of sanction.
