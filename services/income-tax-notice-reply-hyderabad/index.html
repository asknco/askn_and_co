How ASKN & Co. LLP helps

An income tax notice is not something to file away. Every notice carries a deadline, and a missed or poorly drafted reply can turn a simple query into an addition, a demand and penalty proceedings. ASKN & Co. LLP reviews the notice, reconciles your records with the department's data (AIS, TIS and Form 26AS), and drafts a reply that sets out the facts and the legal position clearly.

We handle notices for salaried individuals, NRIs, business owners, firms, LLPs, companies and trusts. Most assessments today are faceless, so we respond on the income tax e-filing portal on your behalf, wherever you are. Notices for earlier years continue under the Income-tax Act, 1961, while the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from tax year 2026-27; we work under both.

Notices we handle

  • Scrutiny assessment notices — section 143(2) and 142(1)
  • Reassessment and escaped-income notices — sections 148 and 148A
  • Defective return notices — section 139(9)
  • Intimations and adjustments under section 143(1)
  • Outstanding demand notices and rectification under section 154
  • Penalty proceedings for under-reporting and misreporting
  • AIS / high-value transaction mismatches and e-verification
  • TDS / TCS default notices and short-deduction demands
  • Faceless assessment and video-hearing representation
  • Post-search and survey assessments

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What should I do first when I receive an income tax notice?

Note the reply deadline, check which section the notice is issued under and do not ignore it. Share the notice and your return with us — we will tell you what it means and what documents are needed, usually within one working day.

How much time do I get to reply to an income tax notice?

The deadline is stated in the notice itself and is often between 7 and 30 days. Where more time is genuinely needed, an adjournment can be requested on the portal, but it should be sought before the deadline expires.

Can you reply to faceless assessment notices for clients outside Hyderabad?

Yes. Faceless assessments are handled entirely online through the income tax portal, so we represent clients across India. Clients in Hyderabad can also meet us at our Madhapur office.

What if the assessment order goes against me?

You can appeal to the Commissioner of Income Tax (Appeals) and, if required, to the ITAT. We also help with stay of demand while the appeal is pending. See our income tax appeals page.

Do I need to visit your office?

No. Most notice work is done over email, phone and WhatsApp. You are welcome to meet the partners at our Madhapur office, close to HITEC City and Inorbit Mall.
