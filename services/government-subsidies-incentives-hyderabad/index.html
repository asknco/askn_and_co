How ASKN & Co. LLP helps

New and expanding enterprises in Telangana can qualify for significant incentives under the state's industrial policy — such as investment subsidy, interest subsidy, power cost reimbursement, reimbursement of stamp duty and land conversion charges, and SGST reimbursement — with enhanced benefits for micro and small enterprises and for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs. Central schemes such as PMEGP, CGTMSE and Stand-Up India add credit-linked subsidies and collateral-free finance.

These benefits are frequently lost not because a business is ineligible, but because registrations were not taken before investment, project costs were not documented in the required form, or claims were not filed within the prescribed time after commencement of production. ASKN & Co. FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What subsidies are available for new industries in Telangana?

Telangana's industrial incentive schemes offer benefits such as investment subsidy, interest subsidy, power cost reimbursement, reimbursement of stamp duty and land conversion charges, and SGST reimbursement. Micro and small enterprises and SC/ST and women entrepreneurs receive higher benefits. Rates and conditions depend on the scheme in force, the size and sector of the enterprise and its location.

What is the PMEGP subsidy?

PMEGP is a central government credit-linked subsidy scheme for setting up new micro enterprises. A margin money subsidy of 15% to 35% of the project cost is available, depending on the location and the entrepreneur's category, on a loan sanctioned by a bank.

Is Udyam registration required to claim MSME benefits?

Yes. Udyam registration is the basis for most MSME schemes and protections, including priority-sector lending and protection against delayed payments. Registration is online and free.

When should I apply for a government subsidy?

Before you invest. Many schemes require registration or approval before the project starts, and claims must usually be filed within a prescribed period after commercial production begins. Late claims are often rejected.

Do you guarantee that the subsidy will be sanctioned?

No. Sanction is decided by the government department under the scheme's conditions. We make sure eligibility is established, the application and claims are complete and on time, and queries are answered promptly.
