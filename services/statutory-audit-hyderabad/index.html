FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is a statutory audit mandatory for a private limited company?

Yes. Every company registered under the Companies Act, 2013 must have its accounts audited by a Chartered Accountant every year, regardless of turnover or profit.

When is a tax audit required?

A tax audit is required when business turnover or professional receipts cross the limits prescribed under the Income-tax Act, with higher limits available where cash transactions are within 5% of the total. We will check whether it applies to you for the year.

Can the same firm do our statutory audit and tax audit?

Yes. The statutory auditor commonly also carries out the tax audit, which saves time because the same records are examined once.

Do you audit LLPs and trusts?

Yes. We audit LLPs, partnership firms, societies and charitable trusts, including audits required for registration and exemption purposes.
