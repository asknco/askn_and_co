FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need GST registration?

Registration is required once your aggregate turnover crosses the threshold that applies to your state and to whether you supply goods or services, and in certain cases — such as inter-state supplies of goods or selling through e-commerce operators — regardless of turnover. Tell us about your business and we will confirm whether you need to register.

How long do I have to reply to a GST show cause notice?

Show cause notices usually allow 30 days for a reply. It is important to reply in writing with documents and to ask for a personal hearing, because the reply forms the record for any later appeal.

What is the time limit for a GST appeal?

An appeal to the first Appellate Authority is generally filed within three months of the order, with a mandatory pre-deposit of a part of the disputed tax. Further appeals lie to the GST Appellate Tribunal.

Can you handle monthly GST filings for a small business?

Yes. We offer monthly retainers that cover returns, reconciliation and routine queries, so you have one team handling GST throughout the year.

Why is my ITC in GSTR-3B different from GSTR-2B?

Differences usually come from suppliers filing late, invoices booked in a different period, blocked credits under section 17(5) or errors in the supplier's return. Our reconciliation identifies each difference and what to do about it.
