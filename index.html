About ASKN & Co. LLP

ASKN & Co. LLP is a full-service Chartered Accountants firm headquartered in Madhapur, Hyderabad, near HITEC City. With over two decades of practice, the firm offers end-to-end expertise spanning Tax Planning and Litigation, GST Advisory, Statutory Audits, Company Law, International Tax, and Virtual CFO services.

The firm is built on a commitment to precision, confidentiality, and outcome-driven advisory — serving individuals, family offices, and corporates alike.

FAQ

Questions about working with our CA firm in Hyderabad

Where is ASKN & Co. LLP located?

Our office is at C1, Spaces & More Business Park @ Madhapur # 2, Plot # 27, Shilpi Enclave, Durgam Cheruvu, Inorbit Mall Road, Madhapur, Hyderabad 500081 — close to HITEC City, Jubilee Hills, Gachibowli and Kondapur. We are open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

What services does your Chartered Accountants firm offer?

We handle income tax notices and assessments, income tax appeals before CIT(A) and ITAT, ITR filing and tax planning, GST compliance and litigation, statutory and tax audits, company registration and ROC compliance, international tax and transfer pricing, accounting, payroll and virtual CFO services, bank loan assistance and project finance, and government subsidies and incentives.

I have received an income tax or GST notice. Can you help urgently?

Yes. Share the notice with us on WhatsApp or email and a partner will review it, explain what it means and tell you the deadline and documents needed — usually within one working day.

Do you work with clients outside Hyderabad?

Yes. Income tax assessments and appeals are largely faceless and GST and company law filings are online, so we act for clients across India and for NRIs abroad. Clients in Hyderabad can also meet the partners at our Madhapur office.
